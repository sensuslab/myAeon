# myAeon Product Information Sheet

## Product Summary

myAeon is a premium cosmic guidance app that combines a live 3D planetary system with AI-generated astrological readings. It turns birth details and selected sky dates into a beautifully presented, reflective reading across love, purpose, body, and inner life.

The experience is designed to feel less like a horoscope feed and more like a personal celestial interface: visual, immersive, calm, and emotionally intelligent.

## Short Promo Copy

Meet myAeon - your cosmic astrological guide.

Explore a live planetary system, cast a personalized reading, and understand what each planetary position means for your current or upcoming sky. myAeon blends real astronomical positioning with poetic AI interpretation, creating readings that are grounded, specific, and easy to return to.

## One-Line Positioning

An immersive astrology companion that transforms planetary positions into beautifully designed personal readings.

## Core Promise

myAeon helps people reflect on timing, energy, and personal direction through a calm visual interface and a structured AI reading system.

## Key Features

- Live 3D planetary visualization with clearer orbit rings, labels, and interactive planet selection.
- Personalized reading generation based on birth date, birth time, optional birth place, and selected sky date.
- Future-date sky estimation for upcoming symbolic readings and preparation.
- Reading sections across four life domains: Love & Connection, Purpose & Work, Body & Energy, and Inner World.
- Timeframe structure covering Today, 3 Days, Week, and Month.
- Planet-by-planet insights unlocked after a reading is generated.
- Light and dark modes with branded myAeon visuals.
- Mobile-first reading drawer and full-screen reading mode.
- Designed PDF export for saving or sharing the complete reading.
- Branded app icon, wordmark, and animated preloader.

## Reading Intelligence

The reading engine enriches AI prompts with:

- Current or selected-date planetary positions.
- Sun sign metadata: element, modality, and ruling planet.
- Major aspects between the selected sky and the user's sun sign.
- Planetary dignity notes such as domicile, exaltation, detriment, and fall.
- Clear safety framing: astrology is treated as a reflective symbolic lens, not a deterministic prediction.

## Audience

myAeon is for astrology-curious users who want something more elegant and personal than generic horoscope apps. It suits people who enjoy reflective tools, visual interfaces, spiritual self-inquiry, and beautifully packaged personal insights.

## Experience Pillars

- Immersive: the planetary system is the first interface, not a decorative background.
- Personal: readings are generated around the user's details and selected sky date.
- Grounded: the prompt avoids fate claims and anchors interpretation in actual supplied astrological context.
- Beautiful: every stage, from preloader to PDF export, is designed as part of the brand.
- Useful: readings include concrete reflection, not vague positivity.
